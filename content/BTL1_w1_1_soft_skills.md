# Security Fundamentals; Soft Skills

#### Communication

All roles in cybersecurity require a high level of both written/verbal communication.

This skill can be used from everyday action such as writing & replying to emails, talking to your team members and internal/external stakeholders to writing up reports and investigation reports.

(Sometimes even non-technical audiences, so explaining your professional idea to someone who doesn't have same technical background as you would be extremely beneficial skill to have!)

* Online Communication
	* Be aware of corporate policies that restrict how electronic communication is used, monitored, and stored 
	* Chat history can be audited & stored 
* Face-to-Face
	* tldr; Don't be a dick and be professional!
	* Always make sure it's not inappropriate & doesn't breach any workplace ethics or policies
- External Parties
	- Keep in mind that you are representing your company - act accordingly.
	- **NEVER EVER give ANY information unless I'm permitted to disclose it to external parties**, if unsure, **ASK**
- Social Engineering
	- I am valuable - I know inside information that outsiders don't 
		- internal IP address
		- tools they use
		- team members etc....
	- **please please PLEASE don't share photos of your work ID badge online!**


Oh I love this rule, **K.I.S.S**
a.k.a. **Keep It Simple Stupid**

It's often better to talk dumb to someone than use jargon and specialist vocabulary that they won't understand, which could give you a wrong answer and take up more time.


#### Teamwork
In cybersecurity, it's all about people working together.
- Go to conferences
- Do CTFs
- Teambuilding exercises with your team & spending time with colleagues



#### Problem Solving

Problems happen everyday - things break, new threats arise, and attack occur.

Hence, working in security involves lots of triaging security events, identifying problems, and working with others to get them addressed. 

Being able to solve a problem in effective & timely manner is GREAT.

- Analysing (e.g. deductive reasoning, game theory...etc)
- Deciding (to choose and implement a solution)
- Managing emotion (improve my own and team members' ability to think clearly)

How to improve problem solving skill?
- Online puzzles and logic-based games
- CTF
- PentesterLab, TryHackMe, OverTheWire.....etc

#### Mental Health

Due to nature of cybersecurity roles, it is totally possible to develop and experience imposter syndrome and burnout.

**Mitigations for professional burnout**
- Job rotation
- New projects
- A strong relationship between management and employees
